How to Play

Guess the daily secret 5-letter word using alphabetical order.

Each guess tells you whether the secret comes before or after it in the dictionary, and a distance % showing how close you are (smaller = closer). Narrow it down like a binary search.

You have 14 guesses. Your score will be based on the number of guesses it took to find the word.
