How to Play

Solve 32 Wordle puzzles at the same time!

Each guess applies to all 32 grids. You have 37 guesses to solve them all.

Green — correct letter, correct position
Yellow — correct letter, wrong position
Gray — letter not in word

The on-screen keyboard shows the best status for each letter across all grids. Tap a grid to filter the keyboard to just that word — tap again to go back to the global view.
